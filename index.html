How to order

Four steps from photo to wrist

  1. Choose a design

    Pick a set from the collection, or send us a photo of the look you have in mind.

  2. Send your size and colour

    Message us on WhatsApp or by email with the design, your bangle size and the colour you want.

  3. Confirm the details

    We reply with the price and when your set will be ready.

  4. Receive your bangles

    Collect from Saidabad, Hyderabad, or ask us about delivery to your city.

About us

Small studio, made by hand

Moura Creations is a small bangle studio in Saidabad, Hyderabad. Each bangle is wrapped in silk thread by hand, then finished with kundan stones, mirror work or gold-tone coins.

We make sets for brides, festivals and gifts, and simple pairs for every day. If you have a saree or outfit to match, send us a photo and we'll suggest colours.
